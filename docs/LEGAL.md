Morphy Patches Creator is published for personal customisation, security research, interoperability work and education. It is not a piracy tool and is not intended to defeat paid access, DRM, or third-party intellectual property.

## 2. Reverse engineering for interoperability

Where applicable, reverse engineering of lawfully obtained software for interoperability is recognised, for example:

- **United States** — 17 U.S.C. § 1201(f)

This is not legal advice. Check your local law and the terms of service of the app you modify before using the tool.
