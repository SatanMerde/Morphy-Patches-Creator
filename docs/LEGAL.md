# ⚖️ Legal, Ethical & Regulatory Framework

## 1. Research & Educational Scope
Morphy Patches Creator is authored strictly for academic security analysis, software interoperability evaluation, and personal device customization. It provides researchers, developers, and educators with tools to understand Android application structures, runtime behavior, and bytecode ergonomics.

## 2. Reverse Engineering for Interoperability
Under international software law:
* **United States (17 U.S.C. § 1201(f))**: Reverse engineering of legally obtained computer programs is permitted for the sole purpose of identifying and analyzing elements necessary to achieve interoperability with independently created computer programs.
* **European Union (Directive 2009/24/EC, Article 6)**: Reproduction of code and translation of its form are permitted without author authorization where indispensable to obtain the information necessary to achieve the interoperability of an independently created computer program with other programs.

## 3. Prohibited Uses
Users of Morphy Patches Creator are explicitly prohibited from using this toolkit to:
* Bypass paid authentication paywalls or commercial licensing mechanisms.
* Conduct unauthorized surveillance, spyware implantation, or malicious payload distribution.
* Violate commercial DRM or copyright protections contrary to applicable laws.
* Infringe upon proprietary intellectual property or redistribute copyrighted binaries.
